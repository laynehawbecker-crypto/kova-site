Support

Questions, problems or ideas? We read every message.

Contact us

Email kovaapphelp@gmail.com, or in the app go to Settings › Send Feedback (it adds your app version and iPhone model so we can help faster). We usually reply within a day or two.

Kova isn't blocking my apps

Make sure Screen Time access is on: iPhone Settings › Screen Time › Apps with Screen Time Access, and Kova is allowed. In Kova, open Screen Time and check your shield shows as on, with the apps you picked. If you use the free plan, only one shield runs at a time; paused shields say so. Still stuck? Open lull://diagnostics in Safari on your iPhone and email us what it shares.

How do I take a break from a shield?

On the shield screen, tap I need a minute, or open the shield in Kova and scroll to Unlock for a bit. Locked shields only open for emergencies.

What's free, and what's in Kova Pro?

Free includes 2 habits, 1 shield, levels, ranks, themes, achievements and this week's report. Kova Pro adds unlimited habits and shields, Locked strictness, saved app sets for Go Quiet, widgets, alternate app icons and all your weekly, monthly and yearly reports.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. To cancel, open iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Kova Pro. If you cancel, nothing is deleted: 2 habits and 1 shield keep running and the rest pause until you come back. To restore on a new iPhone, open Kova's paywall and tap Restore.

How do I delete my data?

Everything Kova stores stays on your iPhone. Use Settings › Erase Data in Kova, or delete the app.
