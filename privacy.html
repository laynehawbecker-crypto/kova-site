Privacy Policy

Updated November 18, 2026

The short version

Kova has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Everything you put in the app stays on your iPhone. We don't collect, sell or share your personal data.

What stays on your phone

Your habits, check-ins, scores, levels, achievements, shields and settings are stored on your device, in storage shared only with Kova's own widgets and Screen Time extensions. They aren't sent anywhere. Deleting the app, or using Settings › Erase Data, removes them.

Screen Time

Kova uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks to quiet the apps you choose and to show your phone time. Apple gives Kova anonymous tokens for the apps and sites you pick, not their names, and usage reports are drawn by iOS on your device. Kova can't see which apps or websites you use, and that information never leaves your phone.

Notifications

Reminders and "I need a minute" alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification content passes through a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. Kova only learns whether your subscription is active; it never sees your payment details. Manage or cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Ratings and feedback

If you rate Kova, Apple shows its own rating prompt. If you send feedback, your email app opens a message you can review before sending; it includes your app version, device model and iOS version so we can help. We use your email only to reply to you.

Children

Kova isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, at any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date. Because Kova doesn't collect data, a change can't apply to anything collected before it.

Questions

Email kovaapphelp@gmail.com, or use Settings › Send Feedback in the app.
